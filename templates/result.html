📚 MRI Class Explorer

General educational information about the categories used by the research model.

🧠 Glioma

Glioma is a group of tumors that arise from glial cells in the nervous system. The model treats it as one classification category.

🧠 Meningioma

Meningioma is a tumor that develops from the meninges, the protective layers around the brain and spinal cord.

🧠 No Tumor

This category represents MRI images in the dataset that are labelled as not containing a tumor.

🧠 Pituitary

Pituitary tumors are abnormal growths involving the pituitary gland. The model treats this as a separate classification category.

⚠️ Research & Educational Disclaimer

NeuroScanAI is an educational and research prototype developed for brain MRI image classification. The displayed prediction and confidence represent model outputs and must not be considered a medical diagnosis. Grad-CAM represents model activation patterns and does not prove the actual location or presence of a tumor. Any medical interpretation should be performed by a qualified healthcare professional.
