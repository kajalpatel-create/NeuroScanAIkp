About NeuroScanAI

NeuroScanAI is a student research project exploring the application of Artificial Intelligence and deep learning in brain MRI image classification. The system uses a four-class dataset containing Glioma, Meningioma, No Tumor and Pituitary categories.

Educational Prototype: NeuroScanAI is developed for educational and research purposes. Its predictions are model outputs and should not be considered a medical diagnosis. Clinical decisions must be made by qualified
